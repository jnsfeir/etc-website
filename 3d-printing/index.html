3D Printing Training Courses

About This Program

ETC Lebanon's 3D Printing program teaches students to design physical prototypes using industry-standard 3D modeling software — Rhino or AutoCAD — and export them in STL format for 3D printing. Most of the course is spent mastering the design software, then students learn the full pipeline from digital model to printed object. The program is offered in a blended format: face-to-face at our Dbayeh facility or live online via Zoom. Computers are provided and international Autodesk or Rhino certificates are awarded upon completion. Installment payment plans are available.
