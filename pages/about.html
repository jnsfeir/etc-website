Our Founder

Naji John Sfeir

Autodesk Certified Adobe Certified V-Ray Certified Ohio State University Former ALBA / AUB / LAU / NDU Professor

Naji John Sfeir holds an Industrial & Systems Engineering degree from The Ohio State University and spent years as a professor at Lebanon's leading universities — ALBA, AUB, LAU, and NDU. In February 2005, he founded ETC with a mission to deliver world-class technical training in the region.

Beyond education, Naji is a certified trainer, entrepreneur, photographer, filmmaker, and SCUBA instructor. That breadth defines ETC's culture of well-rounded, deeply practical learning.

ETC is officially licensed by Lebanon's Ministry of Education (DGTVE), Autodesk and Rhino Authorized, and Adobe Certified. Students completing structured programs receive government-attested certificates. First session is always free — no commitment required.

Project History

Notable Real-World Work

2014

Bahrain Airport Extension — BIM management for ETEC. Full-discipline Revit modeling for one of the Gulf's largest infrastructure projects.

2015

ABC Mall Verdun, Beirut — In partnership with MAN Enterprise. LOD 500 modeling covering architecture, structure, electrical, and mechanical systems.

2017

USJ University Medical Building — Full-discipline BIM modeling for Saint Joseph University's medical faculty complex in Beirut.

2018

U.S. Embassy Compound, Lebanon — A $1 billion project led by BL Harbert (USA). Architectural and reinforcement BIM. 10 ETC-trained graduates were hired directly onto the project.

2020

King Faisal Academy, Saudi Arabia — Full-discipline BIM management for client ACE. 32 ETC graduates were employed on this project.

2023

Cameroon Central Bank — BIM management project for Rafik Khoury & Partners, demonstrating ETC's reach across three continents.
