FAQ

Common Questions

Is the first session really free?
Yes. You can attend the first two sessions of any course at no charge and with no commitment. Evaluate the instructor, curriculum, and environment before deciding to register.
What languages are courses taught in?
English, Arabic, and French. Most courses are offered in English, but Arabic delivery is available for many courses. The online AI chatbot also supports all three languages.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Three-installment payment options are available. Certificates are issued regardless of final payment status. International students can pay via Western Union.
Can I attend online instead of in-person?
All Lebanon campus classes are also available live via Zoom at the same schedule and pricing. The online experience is interactive — not recorded replays.
What if I miss a session?
Free course repetition (online or in-person) is available within one year of registration. Most courses are also recorded for enrolled students to review at their own pace.
Can ETC train employees at our office?
Yes. ETC offers on-site corporate training anywhere in the world — Lebanon, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Africa, Europe, USA. Contact us for group rates and custom scheduling.
How do I get an Autodesk certificate?
Complete the relevant Autodesk course track and take the official Autodesk certification exam. As an Authorized Training Center, ETC can administer and facilitate these internationally recognized exams.
What is the DGTVE certificate?
ETC is registered with Lebanon's Directorate of Vocational and Technical Education (DGTVE), Ministry of Education. Students completing approved multi-course programs can receive government-attested certificates. Processing is pending final approval.
