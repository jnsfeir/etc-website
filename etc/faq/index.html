Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about training at ETC.

Is ETC a licensed school?

ETC is officially licensed by the Lebanese Ministry of Education under the Directorate of Vocational and Technical Education (DGTVE).

What kind of certificates do you issue?

International certificates from Autodesk (AutoCAD, Revit, 3DS Max, Civil 3D) and Rhino are offered. Other courses receive ETC Certificates of Completion.

Do you issue ministry-accredited certificates?

Yes, through multi-course programs. Government processing follows official timelines while students can use ETC certificates immediately.

How do you schedule courses?

Evening classes meet Mondays/Wednesdays or Tuesdays/Thursdays (6:30–9:00 PM), with morning sessions (10:00 AM–1:00 PM) and occasional Saturday classes available.

Do you offer private sessions?

Yes, private sessions are arranged based on individual availability and billed hourly at premium rates.

Are recorded sessions available?

Yes. Students access recordings for review; recording or sharing sessions is prohibited per copyright regulations.

Are make-up sessions provided?

Recorded sessions cover missed material. Students may repeat courses free within one year of registration.

Can students try courses first?

Yes, the first two sessions are offered free before formal registration.

Do payment plans exist?

Yes, fees divide into three installments. Certificates issue regardless of final payment completion.

Do you provide corporate training?

Yes, on-site training is delivered regularly in Lebanon and internationally.

Do I need to bring my own laptop?

No. Our center is fully equipped with computers. You may bring your own laptop if you prefer.

What if I miss a session?

If you miss any sessions, you may retake the course within one year at no additional cost. Recordings are also provided.
