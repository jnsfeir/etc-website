About Expertise Training Center

Professional, technical, and creative education in Lebanon and the region.

Our Story

Founded February 2005

ETC was founded in February 2005 by engineer Naji Sfeir — an Ohio State University Industrial & Systems Engineering graduate, certified Autodesk instructor, Adobe ACE, and V-Ray Certified Professional. ETC is an Authorized Training Center for Autodesk and Rhino, and officially registered under Lebanon's Directorate of Vocational and Technical Education (DGTVE).

Beyond teaching, Naji is a trainer, entrepreneur, award-winning photographer, filmmaker, and SCUBA instructor. His diverse background shapes ETC's practical, real-world approach to every course.

Specialized Fields

  • Visualization & Design — 3D Visualization, Organic Design, Industrial Design, Photography
  • Technology & Business — Project Management, Animation, Web Design, Coding, AI
