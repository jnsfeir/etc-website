Rhino & Grasshopper Courses

ETC Lebanon is a McNeel Rhino Authorized Training Partner offering the complete Rhino learning path — from NURBS modeling fundamentals through Grasshopper parametric design, jewelry CAD with MatrixGold, architectural massing, and product design. Hands-on courses for designers, architects, and engineers.

Rhino — Level 1

30 hrs

NURBS modeling fundamentals with Rhino 3D — surfaces, solids, and curves.

Grasshopper

20 hrs

Visual programming and parametric design in Grasshopper for Rhino.

Precision jewelry CAD modeling with Rhino for 3D printing and manufacturing.

MatrixGold

20 hrs

Professional jewelry design using MatrixGold — gem settings, shanks, and prongs.

Rhino for Architecture

20 hrs

Architectural massing, facade design, and complex forms in Rhino.

Rhino for Product Design

20 hrs

Industrial and product design modeling with Rhino and rendering.
