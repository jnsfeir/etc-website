Fashion Design Using Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop

Duration
20 hours
Fees
Contact for pricing
Mode
Face-to-face / Online
Method
Hands On

Overview

Unleash your creativity and learn the art of fashion design through hands-on experiences and industry-relevant techniques. The program aims to develop professional skills for bringing design concepts to fruition using industry-standard software.

Who Is This For

Fashion Designers and anyone interested in digital fashion illustration and design.

Prerequisites

Adobe Photoshop Essentials; Adobe Illustrator Essentials

Course Contents

  • Introduction to fashion design fundamentals
  • Sketching and hand illustration techniques
  • Fashion illustration with Adobe Illustrator
  • Creating garment technical drawings
  • Color, pattern, and texture application
  • Fabric rendering techniques
  • Fashion collection design
  • Building a professional design portfolio
