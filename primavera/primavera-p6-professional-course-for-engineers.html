Primavera P6 Professional for Engineers and Planners – Advanced Scheduling

Duration
20 hours
Fees
$450
Mode
Face-to-face / Online

Overview

Take your Primavera P6 skills to the professional level at ETC Lebanon. This 20-hour advanced course covers resource and cost management, critical path analysis, baseline establishment, progress updating, professional dashboards, float path analysis, KPIs, S-curves, and AI integration with Primavera — concluded with a real-world final case study.

Who Is This For

Civil and mechanical engineers, construction managers, planners, and project controllers seeking advanced scheduling and project control expertise.

Prerequisites

Basic understanding of project scheduling concepts; familiarity with Excel is highly recommended

Course Contents

  • Environment and Project Setup
  • WBS and Activities
  • Relationships Constraints and CPM
  • Resources Costs and Mapping Files
  • Baselines and Progress Updating
  • Reporting and Dashboards
  • Float Paths and Schedule Analysis
  • KPIs and S-Curves
  • AI With Primavera
  • Final Case Study
