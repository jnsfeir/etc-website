Overview

Master AI-powered visual design, image generation, video production, branding, and content strategy. This hands-on course helps you create professional social media content using prompt engineering, Midjourney, VEO, Flow, Runway ML, and CapCut.

Who Is This For

Designers, architects, interior designers, marketers, branding specialists, content creators, photographers, videographers, social media managers, and anyone who wants to produce professional AI-enhanced visual content.

Prerequisites

Basic computer knowledge. Familiarity with design, content creation, or social media is helpful but not required.
